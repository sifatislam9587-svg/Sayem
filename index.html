Browser extension

AI that captures, enriches and follows up on leads, all from your browser.

Leadloop sits in your toolbar and turns any page you browse into a source of qualified leads. Save a prospect in one click, let the AI fill in and score the details, and keep every conversation moving until it closes.

Why teams use it

  • Save hours each week on manual data entry
  • Reply to warm leads faster, while interest is high
  • Keep one clean, AI-updated source of truth in your CRM
  • Run a repeatable outreach process your whole team can follow

  • One-click lead capture

    Save a prospect from any profile, directory, or company page straight from your browser toolbar.

  • AI enrichment

    An LLM fills in role, company, and context so every lead is ready to work the moment you save it.

  • Real-time CRM sync

    Pushes new leads and updates to your CRM automatically. No exports, no copy and paste.

  • Automated follow-up loops

    AI-drafted reminders and sequences so no warm lead goes cold in your pipeline.

  • AI lead scoring

    Ranks prospects by fit and activity so you always know who to contact next.

  • Private by design

    Runs with minimal permissions and keeps your data in your own workspace.
